# Quillside 0.7.5 — downloadable Mac tester build

Download **Quillside-0.7.5-UNNOTARIZED-macOS-arm64.dmg** from the assets below. Open it and drag Quillside into Applications. A ZIP alternative and individual SHA-256 files are also included.

The package contains the native app, icons, Skills, Pi Agent, and Node runtime. Users do not need Swift, npm, or other build tools. Apple Silicon is the supported distribution architecture.

This is an ad-hoc signed tester build, **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require the [per-app Open Anyway flow](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) after you confirm that you trust the source. Do not disable global system security. App updates may require Accessibility and Notes permissions again.

Changes:

- Produce actual app DMG and ZIP packages by default instead of source-only releases.
- Verify the packaged native executable, signatures, resources, and isolated bundled Pi startup before upload.
- Offer clearly labeled tester downloads on the English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese website.
- Keep source-only and notarized release modes available. Unnotarized downloads never become official notarized downloads.

The app interface remains primarily Chinese. Model accounts are configured by the user. This packaging release does not change Notes editing, model prompts, or writeback behavior. Clean-user installation and broader macOS/IME/multi-display coverage remain separate validation work.
